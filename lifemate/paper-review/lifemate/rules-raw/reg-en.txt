COMPETITION REGULATIONS 
Zalo AI Challenge 2026 
1. General information and effectiveness of the regulations 
1.1. Competition Information 
Competition Name: The official name of the Competition is “Zalo AI Challenge 2026” 
(hereinafter referred to as the “Competition” or “Zalo AI Challenge”). 
Zalo AI Challenge 2026 is a skills competition organized by Zalo AI to promote the fields of 
data science and artificial intelligence in Vietnam. 
1.2. Definitions 
In these Regulations, the following terms shall have the meanings set out below: 
“Organizer” / “Zalo” / “We”: Zalo AI (VNG), the organizing entity responsible for 
implementing, administering, reviewing, and awarding prizes for the Competition. 
“Contestant” / “Participant” / “You”: An individual who meets the eligibility requirements 
under these Regulations and registers to participate in the Competition either individually or as 
a member of a Team. 
“Team”: A group of Contestants formed and participating in the Competition in accordance 
with these Regulations.

the Organizer for research, development, and evaluation purposes within the Competition.

products developed and submitted by a Contestant/Team during the Competition. 
“Competition Code”: Code developed by a Contestant during the Competition, including

These Regulations of the Zalo AI Challenge 2026 (hereinafter referred to as the “Regulations”)

the Competition’s information channels and shall apply throughout the participation, judging, 
and prize-awarding process of the “Zalo AI Challenge 2026”. 
By registering for the Competition, the Contestant confirms that they have read, understood, 
and agreed to comply with all provisions of these Regulations, including the specific rules of

Contestant/Team governing the entire participation, judging, and prize-awarding process of the 
Competition. 
You must agree to and comply with these Regulations in order to submit a Submission and be 
eligible to receive a Prize. 
By participating in the Competition and downloading Competition Data, you agree to comply 
with Zalo’s Terms of Use at https://zalo.vn/dieukhoan/.

2.1. Eligibility 
To be eligible to participate, you must meet all of the following criteria: 
(i) Hold Vietnamese nationality; 
(ii) Be the holder of a valid Zalo Account; and 
(iii) Be at least 18 years old or have reached the age of majority under the laws applicable at 
your place of residence (unless otherwise agreed by the Organizer and you have obtained 
lawful consent from a parent/legal guardian). 
If participating as a Team, the Team Leader must ensure that all Team members have been 
fully informed of and agreed to participate in the Competition under the terms and conditions 
of these Regulations. 
If you participate on behalf of a company, educational institution, or other legal entity as an 
employee, collaborator, or representative, these Regulations shall bind both you and that 
organization. You represent that the organization is aware of and has authorized your 
participation and receipt of any Prize, and that your participation does not violate the 
organization’s internal regulations. 
Ineligible persons: Employees, collaborators, or any individuals who have entered into an 
employment contract, service contract, or are working for VNG and companies within the 
group are not permitted to register for the Competition in any form. 
The Organizer reserves the right to verify eligibility and resolve disputes regarding eligibility 
in cases of suspected violations or at the Organizer’s discretion. 
2.2. Registration and Accounts 
You are not required to purchase anything or make any payment to participate in the 
Competition or receive a Prize. To register, you must create an account on the Competition 
Website before the registration deadline and submit your Submission according to the 
instructions. 
You may participate in the Competition individually or as part of a Team. Each individual may 
register for and participate in the Competition through only one (01) Zalo Account. Each 
member must register individually before joining a Team.

Each Contestant may participate in only one (01) Team or participate individually; a Contestant 
may not simultaneously participate in multiple Teams or participate both individually and as a 
Team member. 
Contestants or Teams may not use multiple accounts, fake accounts, or create multiple Teams 
to submit Submissions and compete for Prizes contrary to these principles. Each Team member 
having a separate account for registration and participation in the same Team in accordance 
with these Regulations shall not be considered a violation. 
2.3. Formation and Management of Teams 
Each Team may have a maximum of five (05) members, including the Team Leader. 
The Team Leader may remove or add members to the Team while continuing to comply fully 
with these Regulations. 
Submissions made by members who have subsequently been removed from the Team shall 
remain counted toward the Team based on the time at which such individual made the 
Submission. 
The Team Leader may request deletion of the Team provided that (1) no members remain in 
the Team and (2) the request is sent to the Organizer at aichallenge@zalo.me. 
2.4. Team Merging 
Team merging is permitted and shall be carried out by the Team Leader. After merging, the 
resulting Team must comply with the member limit and Team-related provisions of these 
Regulations. 
Condition for merging Teams: The total number of Submissions made by the merged Team 
must not exceed the maximum number of Submissions permitted as of the time of merging. 
The maximum number of Submissions shall be calculated as: (Number of Submissions 
permitted per day) × (Number of Competition days elapsed as of the time of merging). 
3. Competition categories and schedule  
The challenge content, datasets, pre-trained models, and specific requirements for each 
challenge shall be published on the portal for each challenge on the Competition Website. 
The Competition consists of two (02) rounds: the public test and the private test, taking place 
from October to December 2026, in the GMT+7 time zone, as follows:

October 26, 2026: Start the challenge. The development kit, data (training & public test), and 
evaluation software are made available. 
November 16, 2026: Submit solutions. All teams submit solutions, including training code, 
testing code, and models. Deadline: 23:59 (GMT+7). 
November 20, 2026: Release private test. The leaderboard is provisional, based on results 
submitted by participating teams and subject to verification. Release time: 9:00 (GMT+7).

updates will be announced on the Competition Website, and Participants are responsible for 
monitoring such updates. 
You must convert the time zone to the applicable time zone at your place of residence to ensure 
timely submission.

delete or destroy Personal Data in accordance with personal data protection laws, data laws, 
and other applicable laws. 
The Organizer makes no representation or warranty regarding the accuracy, ownership, or 
suitability of the data for any purpose other than those stated above.

4.5. Use of Open Source 
If your model uses open-source software, you may only use libraries or source code with a

5. Submissions and submission process 
5.1. Submission Limits and Requirements 
Each Team or individual Contestant may submit a maximum of five (05) Submissions per day 
and may select only one (01) final Submission for official scoring. 
Submissions must comply with the format, technical requirements, and deadlines specified on 
the Competition Website. 
Late Submissions will not be counted. 
A Submission is invalid if it is unclear, incomplete, improperly modified, falsified, or 
fraudulently submitted. 
Contestants/Teams must submit valid Submissions at each stage as required to be considered 
for Prizes. 
5.2. Model Freeze and Final Solution Submission 
Before the private test data is released, the Organizer will require Teams/Contestants to freeze 
their models and submit the final solution, including training code, testing code, model, method 
description, and instructions for running the code. 
All final Submissions will be uploaded to servers designated by the Organizer; technical details 
will be announced before the Competition begins. A model execution time limit will apply to 
the private test data, as specified in the description of each category.

6. Scoring, ranking, and result verification 
6.1. Scoring and Ranking 
Each Submission will be scored and ranked according to the evaluation metric published on 
the Competition Website. During the Competition, the public leaderboard will be updated in 
real time based on results from the public test set (where two Submissions receive the same 
score, the earlier Submission shall rank higher). 
Winners are determined based on the results of the private test round, provided that they fully 
comply with these Regulations. If a winner is disqualified or declines a Prize, the next eligible 
Contestant with the next-highest score will be selected as a replacement. 
6.2. Result Verification 
When results are submitted on the private test data, the Organizer will check and compare them 
with the results generated by the frozen model. 
The Organizer may require potential winners to provide source code and documentation 
demonstrating compliance before confirming the results. 
The official leaderboard will be published on the Competition Website. 
7. Prizes and prize claim procedures 
7.1. Prize Structure 
Each challenge has the following prize structure: 
First Prize for each challenge: VND 90,412,000 in cash (equivalent to USD 3,500*). 
Second Prize for each challenge: VND 64,580,000 in cash (equivalent to USD 2,500*). 
*Converted based on the average buying/selling exchange rate of Vietnam Maritime 
Commercial Joint Stock Bank on October 23, 2026. 
The Organizer will withhold applicable taxes in accordance with Vietnamese law before 
awarding Prizes to winning Contestants. 
Prizes are announced on the Competition Website and are valid only during the specified 
period. The chance of winning depends on the number of valid Submissions and the skill level 
of the Contestants.

7.2. Prize Notification and Acceptance 
Potential winners will be notified by email, telephone, and the Zalo application. 
A potential winner will forfeit the right to receive the Prize if: (i) they do not respond within 
one (01) week from the first contact attempt; or (ii) they notify the Organizer that they do not 
wish to receive the Prize within one (01) week after the Competition ends. 
To receive a Prize, the winner must fully perform the following obligations: 
(a) Submit to the Organizer the final software source code used to create the winning 
Submission, together with documentation, running instructions, and necessary resources; 
(b) Grant the Organizer a license to use the winning Submission in accordance with Section 8 
of these Regulations; 
(c) Sign and return all documents confirming receipt of the Prize, including (i) valid 
certification; and (ii) licenses, minutes, or other undertakings, if any. 
The winner must submit the complete prize claim documents within two (02) weeks from the 
date of notification; otherwise, the Prize will be canceled. The Prize will be awarded within 
thirty (30) days after the Organizer receives all required documents. Prizes are non-transferable 
and may not be assigned. 
7.3. Taxes 
All taxes relating to the Prize are the responsibility of the winner, unless otherwise provided 
by law.
